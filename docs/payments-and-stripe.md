# Stripe Connect: merchant-owned payments embedded in Zackisch

This describes the **release target**, not an assertion that the current sandbox code already implements it. Before publishing this file as operational documentation, implement and verify every statement against the installed Stripe API version and the actual account configuration. Last official-source review: 16 October 2026. Do not treat vendor settings as a substitute for German legal and tax advice.

## Non-negotiable commercial and responsibility model

Zackisch is a restaurant SaaS ordering interface, **not the food seller, acquirer, custodian of consumer funds or legal issuer of the restaurant's fiscal receipt**. The restaurant contracts with its guest for food and service, is identified at checkout, maintains its POS and fiscal duties and is the merchant of record for guest payments. Stripe supplies regulated payment processing and collects the identity, payout account and verification information it requires under its own terms. Zackisch supplies ordering software, onboarding navigation, operations, notices and customer support, and charges the restaurant for SaaS use.

**Preferred and required sandbox candidate:** Connect **Direct Charges on the restaurant's connected Stripe account**, **Stripe-owned payment pricing**, with Stripe collecting processor fees directly from that connected account, Stripe bearing the configured negative connected-account balance exposure, and Zackisch collecting a clearly contracted 2% **application fee**. Never automatically fall back from direct charges to destination charges or platform charges. Never accept user card numbers or upload ID documents to Convex when Stripe can securely collect them.

This is the Stripe-documented SaaS referral-style economic model, not a promise of zero residual liability. Zackisch continues to own its own charge disputes where contractually applicable, platform account negative balances, product availability, software errors, fraud enabled by inadequate authorization, tax invoices for its fees, data protection and security. The restaurant remains responsible for goods, cancellations, consumer complaints and statutory tax books. Stripe handles its processor/KYC duties according to the actual Stripe agreement and account settings.

## Precise connected-account choice

**Select Stripe Accounts v2 Merchant configuration** if supported by the production-capable Stripe SDK/API/region and platform configuration. Create an eligible Germany-based merchant account with:

- `defaults.responsibilities.fees_collector = "stripe"` (connected restaurant is debited Stripe payment fees on direct charges).
- `defaults.responsibilities.losses_collector = "stripe"` (Stripe bears connected-account negative balance exposure according to Stripe's contract; Zackisch's own platform balance exposure remains).
- merchant dashboard `"none"` **only if** this exact combination is supported and the app implements the full account-management/payouts/payments capabilities; otherwise `"full"` is the preferred low-liability fallback, with embedded components inside Zackisch and a visible Stripe Dashboard escalation path. Do not choose `express` solely because it looks more integrated: its controller/fee/liability configuration must be evaluated, and account type is not freely changeable later.
- Stripe-controlled requirement collection, verified from the returned configuration; never create a Custom-style platform-controlled verification and loss-risk model merely to suppress Stripe branding.
- The minimum capabilities needed for selected DE payment methods, transfers and payouts. Never request extra capabilities on speculation.

Accounts v1 **fallback only if Accounts v2 is unavailable for the verified Stripe setup**: create a compatible controller configuration for Stripe-owned fees/losses/requirements, not an Express account with application-owned defaults. Do not mix Accounts v1 and v2 object assumptions. The agent must obtain and save a sanitized *test account configuration matrix* listing actual returned account object keys; freeze the selected architecture before developing the finance UI. If an exact supported low-risk all-in-app combination cannot be verified, prefer verified merchant liability separation over maximum white-labeling, and document the unavoidable Stripe step. Do not represent a dashboard-less account as automatically cheaper or safer.

## Embedded account onboarding: Zackisch-first, Stripe-transparent

Route: `app.zackisch.com/dashboard/[organizationId]/setup/payments` or one canonical wizard equivalent. Owner verifies organization and current region and accepts **Zackisch B2B Terms** separately, with version/time proof. Step explanation in clear German: `Damit Gäste bezahlen können, verbinden wir deinen Betrieb sicher mit Stripe. Stripe prüft die nötigen Unternehmens- und Bankangaben und zahlt Einnahmen an deinen Betrieb aus. Zackisch berechnet 2 % je erfolgreich abgewickelter Bestellung; Stripe-Gebühren fallen zusätzlich an.`

Create the connected account server-side once per authorized legal entity; use stable idempotent provisioning and organization uniqueness rules. Use **Stripe Connect Embedded Components**, preferably `account-onboarding` and `account-management`, displayed within Zackisch via ConnectJS / supported React SDK. Create short-lived **Account Sessions** on authenticated/authorized requests; never persist, log, replay or cache account-session client secrets; deliver only to the verified owner over TLS. Enable only necessary components and features. Review user authentication in embedded components; do **not** disable Stripe authentication to reduce friction without a reviewed equivalently strong control and a supported account/risk configuration. Stripe's KYC forms, verification, mandatory agreement and privacy links must remain authentic and visible. Branding customization should use the original Zackisch mark, Poppins-compatible style and neutral background; never forge Stripe-owned trust marks or hide the fact that Stripe is payment provider.

Offer embedded balance, payout, payment-detail and business-data components **only for supported controller/dashboard configurations**. Do not reimplement bank detail collection or sensitive verification manually. Only if an existing Stripe account linking workflow is both supported and securely documented for the chosen model, add an explicit `Bereits ein Stripe-Konto?` secondary option using Stripe's approved linking/OAuth authorization and verified CSRF state. **Do not implement OAuth by default**; it is not needed just to create and onboard a connected account via Account Sessions.

Authoritative onboarding readiness = Stripe server/API/verified event data, including `charges_enabled`, `payouts_enabled`, capability and pending/overdue requirement status. The embedded onboarding `onExit`, a redirect back, a client-side success screen or an account ID does **not** prove activation. Explain incomplete verification and let the owner resume at any time. If Stripe requires temporary external authentication, preserve wizard progress and return to the exact pending step. Show `Stripe übernimmt die Zahlungsabwicklung` in help/contract, without pretending customers contract with Zackisch for payment processing.

## Guest checkout: restaurant is the seller

- Guest uses the existing QR path (table/pickup); restaurant's verified legal/trading identity and contact are retrievable before commitment; show accurate tax-inclusive item and extra prices and complete order review. Guest needs neither Zackisch nor Stripe account.
- Use Stripe Checkout Sessions with **embedded/custom Elements** compatible with chosen Stripe SDK, while keeping the outer cart, confirmation, order tracker and optional restaurant branding in Zackisch. Stripe-controlled secure card fields, wallets, authentication and mandatory notices may look like Stripe. Never store card PAN/CVV or fake PCI certifications.
- On the backend, build a price/tax snapshot from canonical menu and merchant settings (signed integer cents, VAT type and eligible date). No guest-supplied total, fee, connected account or business identity can be trusted. Create a pending order/payment attempt; use scoped idempotency keys.
- Create a **Direct Charge in the connected account context** (Stripe API request's connected-account/`Stripe-Account` context), with the supported `payment_intent_data.application_fee_amount` equal to the authorized Zackisch fee and exact customer total. Charge belongs to the restaurant connected account, **not the platform**. Do not use `transfer_data.destination` or `amount` for this model. Store Stripe account ID, Checkout Session, PaymentIntent/Charge, application fee and balance transaction IDs with explicit ownership and currency.
- In embedded checkout, make the final purchase control legally unambiguous (e.g. `Zahlungspflichtig bestellen`); display product, price and restaurant seller above it. Apply §312j BGB conservatively to the online guest journey pending counsel review of in-restaurant/remote variations. Do not mislabel a mere `Weiter` as order commitment.
- A return URL or a green payment client callback is **not payment truth**. Deliver an order to the staff terminal only after verified server-side payment completion (properly signed Stripe **Connect webhook** and/or authenticated retrieval in the connected-account context). Verify Stripe event `account`, charge ID, amount, currency and merchant binding. Normalize async/redirect payment methods; handle webhook delays, retries, duplicates and refunds. Distinguish `payment pending`, `paid`, `staff acknowledged`, `POS recorded`, `preparing`, `ready`, `completed`, `partially refunded`, `refunded` and `disputed`, without corrupting historical finance events.
- Sign/verify raw Stripe webhook body, deduplicate event IDs, do not trust metadata to authorize a tenant or region, and reconcile payment reality from connected account balance transactions periodically. Enable relevant Connect events and sandbox cases. Isolate webhook triggers from unauthorized browser requests. The client cannot manipulate fee amounts.
- Avoid claiming that a payment is immediately settled to a bank account: charge success, Stripe available balance and bank payout are distinct states.

Source: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges ; https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout/quickstarts ; https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions ; https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks

## Commercial fee and restaurant money ledger

**Target terms:** 0 EUR Zackisch setup cost, 0 EUR Zackisch monthly base, and a **2.00% Zackisch fee based on the successfully captured restaurant food/drink order value **after discounts and excluding optional gratuities** (guest-tax-inclusive), inclusive of any VAT legally chargeable on Zackisch's own service fee**. Stripe processor fees are **additional** and collected separately by Stripe from the merchant. Do not bill for abandoned, failed or uncaptured payments. Full refunds reverse the corresponding Zackisch fee; partial refunds reverse the same proportion of the original platform fee, unless the legally reviewed merchant agreement specifies a more favorable/other explicit treatment. Partner commissions adjust when the underlying fee is refunded. All amounts derive from canonical cent-level arithmetic, contractual version and real Stripe state; define rounding to integer cents once and regression-test all borders. Do not charge guest an extra fee for using Zackisch without separate legal and product review.

For a paid **EUR 10.00 order** the target **gross** Zackisch fee is EUR **0.20**; if Zackisch is subject to German 19% VAT on its domestic B2B service, the included VAT portion is approximately EUR 0.03 and the net portion approximately EUR 0.17 *at this single-line rounded level*. Do not add a second 19% to the 20 cents; do not equate Stripe application fee amounts with taxable invoice amounts without mapping. For monthly merchant invoices aggregate and calculate tax consistently on the invoiced amounts; evaluate line-level and total rounding with adviser and implementation tests. Stripe fees are independent, variable and shown truthfully in checkout/merchant billing disclosures. This is a *commercial target*; tax-adviser sign-off is required for actual invoice handling (and company tax status/exemptions).

Create distinct immutable ledger columns/events: `guestGross`, restaurant product VAT by rate, `platformFeeGross`, `platformFeeNet`, `platformFeeVat`, `stripeActualProcessingFee`, `merchantStripeBalanceChange`, payout, refunds, fee refunds, disputes, partner commission. Avoid treating all guest proceeds as Zackisch revenue. Do not estimate Stripe fees and present estimates as actuals. Implement monthly, merchant-directed Zackisch B2B fee invoice/credit-note logic, receipt of structured B2B e-invoices when required, archive with applicable retention, reconcilable per transaction and correct tax treatment of Stripe supplier bills. Never generate a guest food invoice in Zackisch's legal name.

## Merchant refund, dispute and operational policy

Restaurant is the default decision maker for food/guest refunds; authorized Zackisch support personnel may perform a documented delegated action when expressly authorized by the merchant and the relevant agreements. Provide secure, auditable full and partial refunds in the merchant connected-account context; preserve original order, payment and POS-recorded status. Any application-fee refund is explicit and idempotent; test whether Stripe processing fees are retained according to actual contract. Surface payment disputes, payout restrictions and required merchant action without implying Stripe resolves every consumer dispute on Zackisch's behalf. Never silently write off unresolved merchant negative balances or claim risk-free operation.

## Sandbox migration instructions for this repository

Since Zackisch uses Stripe sandbox and has no live payment data that must be preserved, **the owner authorizes a breaking refactor of the current sandbox payment architecture**. Build regression fixtures for existing guest/staff/merchant functionality, then deliberately replace noncompliant flow rather than preserving platform-charge compatibility. - `webapp/convex/stripe.ts` uses `stripe.checkout.sessions.create`/`stripe.paymentIntents.create` on the platform and `transfer_data.destination` with the amount net of the Zackisch percentage; replace with connected-account Direct Charge and application fee.
