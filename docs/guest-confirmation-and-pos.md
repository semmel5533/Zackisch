# Restaurant sale, guest confirmation and existing POS workflow

**Release-state document: publish only after implementing and testing.** German launch design reviewed against BMF cash-register FAQs (29 April 2026), § 146a AO, KassenSichV §§ 1, 6, § 14 UStG, § 33 UStDV, § 312j BGB and PAngV § 13. A specialized German fiscal expert must confirm the factual classification of the actual in-restaurant QR/online/standalone POS combination. Zackisch's claim to be a POS *supplement* does not decide the legal classification.

## Clear division of documents

1. **Zackisch guest order/payment confirmation** — immediately available upon server-verified successful payment in app, labelled `Bestell- und Zahlungsbestätigung`, with a conspicuous `Kein Kassenbeleg und keine Rechnung` note until a compliant integrated fiscal or invoice solution is verified. It documents *what happened in Zackisch* and helps the guest/staff locate the purchase; it is not the restaurant's mandated fiscal record by itself. Offer PDF/download without forced email; optional transactional email uses a separate opt-in and privacy legal basis, not marketing consent. Avoid claiming that labelling alone determines legal status; substance and real system behavior matter.
2. **Restaurant POS fiscal receipt / cash-register document** — generated by the restaurant's existing compliant POS when its operator records the paid digital sale with payment type `already paid externally/card via Zackisch` or an accurate equivalent, without collecting money twice. If that POS transaction is within the §146a AO / KassenSichV scope, follow POS/TSE documentation and make the required receipt available to guests in the legally required time and format. Do not issue fictitious/duplicate cash sales or a receipt dated with an inaccurate time. The restaurant is responsible for lawful invoicing and tax ledger completeness.
3. **Restaurant tax invoice** — issued by the restaurant if required/requested under applicable law. It does **not** have to originate technically from the POS, but its issuer, contents, numbering and booking must be correct. Zackisch does not issue a restaurant invoice in Zackisch's own name. The correct business issuer can supply an appropriate invoice outside the POS if law and record consistency permit. A payment processor receipt does not automatically satisfy tax invoice or KassenSichV receipt requirements.
4. **Zackisch B2B invoice** — a separate invoice from Zackisch to the restaurant for Zackisch's software service fee, governed by a separate 2%-inclusive-tax commercial arrangement, potentially structured e-invoice rules and corresponding tax obligations.

Do not invent tax breakdowns, card brands, authorization codes, refund rights, delivery times or bank transfer confirmation. If including item-level tax labels in confirmation, ensure they are exact from the stored payment/order snapshot and not a misleading invoice.

## Canonical 9-step workflow

1. Restaurant registers, accepts Zackisch merchant contract and Stripe embedded onboarding; its legal seller identity is verified/available. Wizard shows prerequisites and includes mandatory `How to book externally paid orders in your POS` guide with an actual demonstration.
2. Restaurant marks and validates its supported POS workflow before activating QR signs. This is **not** proof of legal fiscal compliance; expert classification/certification remains a release gate.
3. Guest scans venue/table QR, sees the restaurant seller, tax-inclusive live menu, product modifiers, applicable information and final binding button `Zahlungspflichtig bestellen` (or legally reviewed equivalent).
4. Guest pays in Stripe-secured embedded payment form. Backend verifies payment owner/amount/currency via signed Connect webhook or trusted connected-context Stripe retrieval and idempotently creates one *paid* staff order. A payment in `processing` is never labelled paid.
5. Zackisch immediately offers guest confirmation and secure live status. Staff terminal receives a prominent ticket with `Bereits online bezahlt` and the payment reference; do not display it as `cash/card payment due`.
6. **POS transfer step:** staff records each paid Zackisch order in the restaurant's own POS **once**, before declaring the order operationally reconciled. Merchant uses actual food/drink tax mapping, exact sum, payment type already settled via Zackisch/Stripe, unique external reference and staff audit trail. Separate POS and Zackisch events; importing from an electronic order system into POS later cannot be silently assumed compliant at every possible time point. Do not use a fake zero-payment cash sale to make two systems match.
7. Staff marks `In Kasse erfasst` after completing it. Store confirmation `posRecordedAt`, authorized `posRecordedBy`, optional masked external POS reference; `paidAt` comes from Stripe. A distinct `needs_pos_recording`/`attention` queue flags overdue/late/missing records with no automatic lying or silent completion. Keep kitchen prep flow available, with POS warnings not hidden. Exact requirement for POS-before-prep or receipt timing must be decided by fiscal adviser.
8. The restaurant issues/offers its required proper POS receipt and any legally necessary tax invoice to the guest. Give staff clear handoff instructions for takeaway customers who leave before printing, and an authorized correction process for errors. No assumption that the Zackisch confirmation discharges the §146a receipt obligation.
9. At closing, export immutable reconciled transactions with order ID, payment timestamp, gross, tax category and POS recorded status, refund/partial refund/reversal, Stripe account charge ID and date. Flag unmatched or duplicate POS entries; preserve records for the legally required period. Show refund/reversal instructions and immutable audit history. Support manual fallbacks on network outage and replay delayed paid orders once connectivity resumes; never erase a settled payment or invent an offline checkout.

## German classification is fact-sensitive

BMF distinguishes electronic systems with and without cash-functionality. A web shop without a payment made **physically on site** may be treated differently from an on-site point of sale; **a guest scanning a table QR in a restaurant is physically present**, so the exact in-venue checkout cannot simply claim the web-shop exception. Fully separate electronic cashless systems may have different requirements if clear separation can be demonstrated. Get written specialist analysis for the concrete flow and any downstream POS interface, including:

- whether Zackisch itself is an electronic recording system with a cash function for this deployment, the effect of physical presence and its combination with existing POS;
- timing of recording, TSE signing, receipt availability and physical/electronic consent, full system documentation, audit exports, DSFinV-K and GoBD obligations;
- double booking prevention and on-site table payments vs pickup pre-order from outside the premises;
- POS receipt presentation electronically/paper, optional email and legally compliant correction after refunds.

**Product gate:** if specialist concludes that a separated cashless on-site QR system still requires TSE for some configurations, do not release those unsupported combinations. Implement compatible TSE/POS integration or narrow allowable operation with clear merchant restrictions. Never rely solely on a customer checkbox `I handle my taxes`.

## Required safeguards and tests

- Test 1- and 2-item charges, different 7/19% tax classes, rounding, discounts, tips and zero amounts (tips must not be silently assumed food VAT); show exact sale gross in both systems.
- Duplicate webhook/double tap/intermittent tablet network => **one** paid transaction + one POS entry; pending/failed payment => **zero** paid tickets.
- Manual POS acknowledgement cannot be toggled by a guest or unauthenticated staff session; every change logged with org/region; mismatched amount flagged, corrections not silently overwritten.
- Test staff prints correct POS ticket, downloadable guest confirmation **not marketed as tax receipt**, restaurant issuer visible, support guide follows real POS setup.
- Test guest leaves before POS receipt, pickup order ready earlier, unrecorded paid orders when Zackisch outages, refunds before and after POS booking, disputes and partial refunds. Document supported legal treatment per case and test operational reconciliation.
