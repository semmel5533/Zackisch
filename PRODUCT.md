# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Restaurant operators evaluating QR ordering for table service and pickup. Guests order and pay on their own phones; kitchen staff receive orders in a browser.

## Product Purpose

zakkig provides QR-based ordering and payment alongside a restaurant's existing workflow. Direct registration is the primary marketing conversion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Menu management, printable QR codes, live kitchen orders and transaction exports. POS entry is manual. No setup or monthly fee; 2 % zakkig platform fee per order plus variable payment-provider fees, confirmed by the user on 2026-09-21. Historical orders retain their recorded fees.

## Brand Commitments

Predominantly white, black type and controls, Poppins text and Inter digits. Consistent spacing, restrained copy and actual product imagery. One physical detail: a QR sheet attached with tape. Existing squirrel mascot in the closing section. No invented metrics, testimonials or decorative SaaS cards.
